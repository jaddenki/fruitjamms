# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential collaborators, clients, and curious visitors who discover jamms through word of mouth or social links. They arrive expecting something with personality — not a corporate studio page. Context: browsing on a laptop, probably evenings, looking for a studio with genuine craft and a distinct point of view.

## Product Purpose

jamms is an indie game / digital studio landing page. Its job is to make one thing land immediately: this studio has taste, they're playful, and they make real things worth looking at. Success means a visitor opens a project before they scroll.

## Brand Personality

Playful, precise, strange. Warm but not cute. Pixel-native. The aesthetic sits at the intersection of 80s arcade culture and contemporary interaction design — it knows both worlds and refuses to choose.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-mode agency portfolios (all neon gradients on black)
- Framer/Webflow template studios (grid of cards, hover scales, gradient text CTAs)
- "brutalist" sites that mistake ugliness for edge
- Anything that looks like it was generated with an AI design tool

## Design Principles

1. **Everything is one surface.** No modals dropped on top, no separate layers. Elements morph and reshape — the pill becomes the panel, the panel becomes the nav.
2. **Pixel as material.** The cross icons and mascot aren't decorative — they're the visual language. They thread through every state.
3. **Black is load-bearing.** The background isn't empty space; it's the stage. The dark pane in the open menu is the background itself, reframed.
4. **Snappy, not precious.** Motion is fast and confident. Ease-out-quart, 300–400ms. No lingering.
5. **Show the work before explaining it.** The overlay opens straight to projects — no "about us" first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduce motion support for the pill-to-panel morph. Tab-navigable nav rail.
